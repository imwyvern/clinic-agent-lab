# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback, consider different
backgrounds and experience levels, and disagree about ideas without attacking people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and sharing private or patient information
are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to wpkreg@gmail.com without including sensitive data.
The maintainer may remove harmful content or restrict participation, proportionate to
the conduct. Good-faith reports should be handled discreetly and without retaliation.
